# Code of conduct

Table Craft adopts the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/). The full text applies to everyone taking part: in issues, pull requests, discussions and any other project space.

## In short

We want this to be a welcoming project for people of every background and level of experience, from first-time contributors to physicists.

**Do:** be kind and patient, assume good intent, give and take feedback on the work rather than the person, and help newcomers find their feet.

**Don't:** harass, insult or demean anyone; make sexualised comments; publish someone's private information; or keep up behaviour after being asked to stop.

## Reporting

If someone's behaviour makes you uncomfortable, report it privately to the maintainers. Use the contact address on the maintainer's GitHub profile ([@agnyaatha](https://github.com/agnyaatha)), or GitHub's [report content](https://docs.github.com/en/communities/maintaining-your-safety-on-github/reporting-abuse-or-spam) tools. Reports are kept confidential.

Maintainers will review every report and respond as the Contributor Covenant's enforcement guidelines describe: from a private warning up to a permanent ban, depending on how serious and how repeated the behaviour is.
