# Three-Cushion

Physics: [physics.md](physics.md). Code: `src/games/threecushion/index.ts`.

## Rules implemented

No pockets and three balls. First to 15 wins.

- A point is your ball hitting both other balls, with at least three cushions touched before the second one.
- Score and you play on. Miss and the turn passes.

The opening position has the red on the foot spot, the yellow on the head spot and the white beside it.

## Not available

No computer opponent or practice-table coach, since those are built around potting. The drills' "show me" search does work here.

## Content

Four drills building from a simple cannon to the full three cushions.
